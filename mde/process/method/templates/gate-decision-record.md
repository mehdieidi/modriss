## Decision rationale

State why the evidence supports the decision and which alternatives were
rejected. Task completion percentage is not sufficient evidence for acceptance.

## Follow-up and trace

Record the next authorized activity, affected backlog/risk/decision records,
and links to the exact decision and evidence revisions.
