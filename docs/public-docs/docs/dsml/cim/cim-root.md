# CIM model root

Source: `mde/metamodels/cim/cim-root.emf`.

`CIMModel` is the root object for one computation-independent model. It gives a transformation a single business scope and a single containment tree. The root does not represent a deployed application. It represents the slice of organisational reality that the current modeling increment is intended to understand and refine.

The root gathers five kinds of information. Its scalar attributes identify the domain and modeling context. Its organization containments hold intent and ownership concepts. Its domain and behavior containments hold the vocabulary of the problem. Its process, policy, governance, and transformation containments hold rules and uncertainty. The optional `traceModel` and `readiness` objects record the evidence needed to review the increment and its generated successors.

## `CIMModel`

Direct supertype: `kernel.TraceableElement`. Inherited identity, explanations, lifecycle, source evidence, review, and trace features are documented in the [shared kernel](../shared-kernel.md).

### Declared attributes

| Attribute          | Type and multiplicity | Meaning in a CIM model                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Example                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `domainName`       | `String` `1`          | Names the business domain that gives the model its vocabulary. It should identify a business area such as municipal grant administration or order fulfilment, rather than a software product or cloud service. EVL requires it, together with `businessScope` and `organizationName`, for a complete model scope. | `Community grant administration`                                                               |
| `businessScope`    | `String` `0..1`       | States what the increment includes and excludes. This prevents a transformation from treating every nearby business concern as part of the current design.                                                                                                                                                        | `Application, review, award, and disbursement of emergency grants; reporting is out of scope.` |
| `organizationName` | `String` `0..1`       | Identifies the organization, department, or business unit whose intent and ownership decisions are being modeled. It provides the institutional context for requirements, actors, capabilities, and governance.                                                                                                   | `Regional Relief Agency`                                                                       |
| `modelingDate`     | `Date` `0..1`         | Records when the current business understanding was captured. It makes the age of assumptions, policies, and stakeholder decisions visible.                                                                                                                                                                       | `2026-04-10`                                                                                   |
| `language`         | `String` `0..1`       | Records the natural language used for the model's names and explanations. This matters when a model crosses teams or is later used to generate documentation.                                                                                                                                                     | `en`                                                                                           |

## Root-level semantic rules

The EVL rules in `mde/validation/cim/rules/core-kernel-trace.evl` require a semantic core: at least one goal, actor, and capability. A complete scope declaration is a warning when `domainName`, `businessScope`, or `organizationName` is missing. Every model element must have a user-facing name and an explanation, and CIM text must avoid provider-specific terms such as Lambda, S3, or CloudFormation.

The root also acts as a policy switch for two cross-cutting checks. If `transformationProfile.requireExplicitActorAuthForCommands` is true, every human-issued command must carry an explicit authorization decision. If `requirePrivacyClassificationForAllInformation` is true, every root-level `InformationItem` must have a `DataClassification`. A model marked production-ready cannot retain blocking hotspots, production-blocking risks, blocking readiness findings, or failed readiness checks.

## Role in CIM-to-PIM refinement

`CIMModel2PIMModel` in `mde/transformations/cim-to-pim/root-scaffolding.etl` creates the PIM root, trace model, and initial readiness assessment from this object. It also carries the model's domain name, scope, organization, and lifecycle information into the PIM. The root therefore supplies the context in which all more specific CIM-to-PIM correspondences are interpreted.
